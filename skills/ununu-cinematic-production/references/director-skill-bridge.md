# Director Skill 05/04/06 bridge

Use the local `script-writing-studio` Director Skill as a production-semantics
source, not as a second runtime or a complete product definition.

## 05 → UnuTV asset authority

Preserve stable IDs and revisions for style, characters, scenes, props,
costumes, lighting, negative constraints, sound, spatial maps, crowd plans and
first-frame requirements. Compile these into `AssetAuthority`,
`ReferenceBinding`, Director Stage and accepted media versions.

Do not stop at an asset table. UnuTV additionally requires:

- exact media ID/checksum and accepted pixel review;
- parent/child asset lineage and Series reuse/freeze rules;
- separation of appearance authority from spatial and temporal control;
- current reference responsibility and Provider eligibility;
- canvas-node projection of every current asset and version.

Scene masters remain empty unless people are part of the accepted fixed world.
Crowd behavior is a timed plan. A key prop uses one-instance custody/contact
tracking. Local crops require a whole-scene locator.

## 04 → UnuTV Shot Intent and previs

Preserve the Director Skill's required focal length, aperture, focus/depth,
camera position, composition, camera behavior, visible performance, exact
dialogue/tone, motivated lighting, bidirectional constraints and next-state
handoff.

UnuTV additionally converts those fields into:

- versioned `CinematicShotSpec` and visible Shot node;
- screen zones, axis, actor/camera paths and contact state;
- numeric camera route, speed/focus curves and start/stop frames;
- low-poly timed previs with top-down, editor and POV views;
- `CutDecision`, `SequencePrevisDocument` and `VisualContextBundle`;
- risk-adaptive acceptance and invalidation.

The 4–15 second Provider segment is not necessarily one artistic Shot. A Shot
may cross Provider segments; a segment may contain several designed Shots.

## 06 → Provider adapter

Use the Director-track prompt shape as an output style when it matches the
Provider. Keep exact dialogue, reference duties, camera behavior, light,
sound, hard locks and end state. Keep parameters outside content prose.

UnuTV additionally requires:

- one deterministic Prompt Draft compiled from accepted structured sources;
- exact ordered media/checksum manifest;
- official model capability validation;
- text/image/annotation and frame-mode conflict audits;
- visible canvas projection of the exact effective request;
- accepted low-cost previs and formal-generation intent before submission;
- idempotent one-submission policy and full-timeline candidate evaluation.

Do not copy internal management IDs into the final Provider content Prompt.
Do not generate BGM in a video prompt unless the selected Provider contract
explicitly assigns music generation; normal BGM belongs to the sound/edit
stage.

## Precedence

Resolve conflicts in this order:

1. Owner-locked story, exact dialogue and release decisions;
2. latest UnuTV production contracts and accepted actual-take canon;
3. accepted pixel Authority and accepted low-poly previs;
4. Director Skill production semantics;
5. Provider-specific optimization.

When the Director Skill omits a required UnuTV gate, add the gate. When it
conflicts with current accepted production truth or runtime capability, block
and record the conflict instead of silently following the template.
